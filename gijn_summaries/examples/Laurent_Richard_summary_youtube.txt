YOUTUBE SUMMARY

In this masterclass, Laurent Richard, founder and director of Forbidden Stories, explains how the Safebox Network protects investigative journalists who are threatened, jailed, or killed for their reporting.

Richard walks through the three steps of the mechanism: journalists securely submit their sensitive, ongoing investigation through an encrypted system built on SecureDrop; they leave instructions on what should happen to their work if they are silenced; and they choose whether to make their membership public as a deterrent. If a journalist is harmed, a network of more than 50 reporters picks up their investigation so the story still gets published.

With over 230 journalists using the network daily, Richard shows how visibility itself protects reporters: several members jailed abroad were released once it became public that Forbidden Stories would continue their work if they weren't. “The story they wanted to silence will be amplified, will be exposed to the rest of the world.”
