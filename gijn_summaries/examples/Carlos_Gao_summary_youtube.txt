YOUTUBE SUMMARY

In this masterclass, Carlos Gao, CEO of Media Defence, a UK-based human rights organization dedicated to the legal protection of journalists, shares three practical tips for facing SLAPPs — strategic lawsuits against public participation designed to harass and censor reporters rather than to win in court.

Gao urges journalists to have their reporting reviewed by a lawyer before publication, to act immediately rather than ignore a threatening letter or lawsuit notice, and, when legal costs are out of reach, to turn to organizations like Media Defence, which can fund emergency defense, connect journalists to a trusted lawyer, and support appeals up to international courts.
